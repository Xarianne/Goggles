! ------------------------------------------------------------------
! Universal Blue — a Brave Search Goggle
! ------------------------------------------------------------------
! Goal: when searching topics related to Universal Blue images
! (Bluefin, Aurora, Bazzite, uCore) and atomic/cloud-native Fedora
! desktops, surface the official distro blogs, docs, forums and
! project repos instead of generic Linux listicle coverage.
!
! This is a BOOST goggle: matched sites are raised in the ranking,
! but non-matched results still appear (unmatched results are not
! discarded). If you want a strict "only UB sites" mode, see the
! README.md in this folder for how to add a generic $discard rule.
! ------------------------------------------------------------------
